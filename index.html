Computational Materials Science

Joy Ekka

PhD candidate at Université de Montpellier (Institut Charles Gerhardt Montpellier (ICGM), CNRS), co-funded by CNES. Defending in December 2026.

About

My PhD focuses on multiscale modelling & understanding of composites of metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) and hydrophobic polymers for capture of volatile organic compounds (VOCs).

Before my PhD I trained as a physicist (MSc), applying electronic structure theory to 2D transition-metal dichalcogenides, perovskites, and spinels for different applications.
