MOF/Polymer composites for VOC capture

My core PhD work centers around modelling MOF/Polymer composites.

An integrated multi-scale computational workflow to design and characterize MOF/hydrophobic-polymer composites for selective volatile organic compound (VOC) capture under humid conditions.

Multi-scale pipeline

An end-to-end multiscale framework spanning DFT, GCMC, MD, and graph-theory pore-network analysis — including accessible free-volume profiling and void-grid community detection to quantify how infiltration reshapes the pore topology thereby identifying best overall balance between VOCs uptake, adsorption energetics and molecular transport.
